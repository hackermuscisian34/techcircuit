AURORA LABS BRAND GUIDELINES (Rulebook Compilation Fixture)

1. Brand Voice
Use a clear, friendly, confident voice. Avoid exaggerated promises and aggressive sales language.
Do not describe the product as 'revolutionary', 'perfect', or 'guaranteed to work for everyone'.
Prefer short sentences and explain technical terms when writing for new users.

2. Approved Terminology
The product name is Aurora Desk. Always use this exact spelling and capitalization.
Use 'team workspace' instead of 'collaboration hub'.
Write 'sign in' as two words. Do not use 'login' as a verb.

3. Claims and Evidence
Never claim 100% uptime unless an approved, current source explicitly supports the exact claim.
Performance claims must include the tested environment and measurement period.
Customer counts, savings, and percentages must match the current Approved Facts Sheet.

4. Visual Identity
Primary brand orange is #F97316. Deep orange is #C2410C. Warm background is #FFFAF5.
Use the approved Aurora Labs logo without stretching, recoloring, or placing it on a visually busy background.
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the logo's symbol.

5. Accessibility
Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images.
Do not communicate status using color alone; include a text label or icon with an accessible name.
Links must describe their destination. Avoid link labels such as 'click here'.

6. Publication Rules
Blocking: unsupported factual claims, incorrect product name, or prohibited absolute guarantees.
Warning: terminology drift, overly aggressive tone, or missing image alternative text.
Advisory: long sentences or optional opportunities to improve clarity.
