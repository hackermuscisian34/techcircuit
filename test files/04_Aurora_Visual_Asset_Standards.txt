AURORA LABS VISUAL ASSET STANDARDS

Brand Color Palette:
- Primary Orange: #F97316 (Primary buttons, accents, icons)
- Deep Orange: #C2410C (Headings, active states)
- Warm Background: #FFFAF5 (Page canvas, containers)
- Dark Ink: #21160F (Body text, contrast ratio > 7:1)

Logo Specifications:
- Master Mark Aspect Ratio: 1:1 (Square icon) or 3:1 (Horizontal wordmark)
- Clear Space Margin: Minimum 24px padding on all sides
- Prohibited Modifications: Stretching, squishing, altering opacity, applying drop shadows
